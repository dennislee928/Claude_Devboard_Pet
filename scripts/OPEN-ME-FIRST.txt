DevPet — first launch on macOS
==============================

DevPet is signed with its own certificate ("DevPet Project"), not with an
Apple-issued Developer ID, because that requires a paid Apple Developer
account. macOS therefore does not trust the signature on first launch.

Drag DevPet.app to Applications, then:

  1. Right-click (or Control-click) DevPet in Applications and choose "Open".
  2. Click "Open" again in the dialog.

If macOS refuses outright (Sequoia and later), go to
System Settings -> Privacy & Security, scroll to Security, and click
"Open Anyway" next to DevPet.

Or, from a terminal:

  xattr -d com.apple.quarantine /Applications/DevPet.app
  open /Applications/DevPet.app

DevPet runs with no Dock icon and no menu-bar item by design — look for the
pet itself on your screen. Right-click the pet for its menu.

To install the command-line pieces (pet-hook, which the Claude Code hooks
call) use the .pkg instead: double-click "Install DevPet.command", or run

  sudo installer -pkg DevPet-<version>.pkg -target /
